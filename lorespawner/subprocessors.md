# Lore Spawner Subprocessor List

**Effective date:** 21 September 2026  
**Provider:** Kaperkunde  
**KVK number:** 42061326  
**Address:** Prof. E.M. Meijerslaan 1, Amstelveen, The Netherlands  
**Privacy/GDPR requests:** privacy@kaperkun.de  

This list names everyone other than Kaperkunde who touches Lore Spawner data, and what each one
is for. Company-wide subprocessors are listed in
[§5.1 of the Privacy Policy](../privacy.md#51-when-kaperkunde-is-controller); the ones below are
Lore Spawner's own.

It is a short list on purpose. Everything the service is made of — the database, the GraphQL
API, the mail server, the app itself — is Kaperkunde's own software, running on servers
Kaperkunde rents. Nothing is delegated to a platform.

---

## Current subprocessors

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hetzner Online GmbH** | The servers Lore Spawner runs on: the application, the database, and outbound mail | European Union. The only processor with access to stored data. |
| **Google (FCM)** | Relaying push notifications to Chrome and Android browsers, where a player has turned them on | Relay only. The payload is encrypted to keys only that browser holds, so the service cannot read what it passes on. |
| **Apple (APNs)** | Relaying push notifications to Safari, where a player has turned them on | Relay only, on the same terms. |
| **Mozilla (autopush)** | Relaying push notifications to Firefox, where a player has turned them on | Relay only, on the same terms. |

The three push services are named for completeness rather than because they hold anything. A
push message is encrypted under RFC 8291 to a key pair generated by the player's own browser;
the service is handed ciphertext and an address, and can read neither the message nor the
account behind it. A player who never turns notifications on never reaches them at all.

---

## What this list does not cover

**An AI assistant a player connects themselves.** Lore Spawner can be connected to an AI
assistant over OAuth. That assistant acts as the player who allowed it, under limits they set,
and what it does with what it reads is between them and whoever makes it. It is the player's
relationship, not Kaperkunde's, and it is not a subprocessor of ours. A connection can be
withdrawn at any time from the AI page in the account.

## Changes to this list

Lore Spawner may add or replace subprocessors as needed to run the service. Where required by
applicable data-protection law, Kaperkunde will give reasonable notice of material changes.

Adding one is a change to this file and to the
[Lore Spawner Privacy Notice](privacy.md) in the same breath — the notice says there is one
processor, and a second would make that untrue.

To ask questions or object to a material change, contact **privacy@kaperkun.de**.
