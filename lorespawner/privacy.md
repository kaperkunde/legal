## What we hold

- **Your account.** The email address you signed up with, a display name if you set one, and
  your password — stored hashed, so nobody at Kaperkunde can read it. Sign-in sessions, email
  verification and password reset records sit alongside it.
- **What you create.** The worlds, places, characters, events and writing you put into Lore
  Spawner, and who contributed what. This is the substance of the service and is kept until you
  remove it or close your account.
- **Who you are playing with.** Invitations you send or accept, and which characters have been
  handed to whom.
- **Notification settings, and what has been sent to you.** The notifications the app has
  written for you, how far you have read, and how you want to be told about each kind of thing.
- **Devices you turn notifications on in.** If you say yes to notifications in a browser, we
  keep the address that browser's push service issued us and the keys that encrypt a message to
  it. There is one such record per browser, and forgetting a device deletes it.
- **Whether you are here.** A single timestamp of when you last did something in the app, so we
  can stay quiet rather than mail you about something you are watching happen. It is
  overwritten, not accumulated: there is no history of your visits.
- **What you have said to the AI.** If this campfire has a language model and you talk to it
  about one of your worlds, the conversation is kept so you can come back to it. Unlike an
  event, it was never written to be read by anybody else, and nobody else can: it is yours and
  the model's.
- **What the AI has cost.** A line per call saying who it was for, which world it was about,
  what kind of work it was, and how many tokens it took. It is a meter, so that a runaway agent
  is visible and can be capped; it holds nothing of what was written.
- **Ordinary server logs.** Requests to the site, with IP address and timestamp, kept briefly so
  we can fix things that break and stop people abusing the service.

That is the whole list. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel and no
profiling of you anywhere in Lore Spawner. The only cookies we set are the ones that keep you
signed in, which is why you have never been asked to dismiss a cookie banner here.

### If somebody invites you

Inviting a friend means giving us their email address so we can send them one invitation. We use
it for that and nothing else, we do not add it to any list, and if they decline — or never
answer — the invitation can be taken back, which deletes the address with it.

If you got an invitation from us and had never heard of Lore Spawner, that is where your address
came from, and **privacy@kaperkun.de** will remove it on request.

## Who else ever sees it

**We do not sell your data, share it, rent it, give it away, or hand it to advertisers, data
brokers or AI training sets.**

**One processor.** Lore Spawner runs on servers rented from [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com),
in the European Union. Everything else the service is made of — the database, the API, the mail
server — is Kaperkunde's own software running on those servers. No analytics service, no content
delivery network, no mail provider, and none of your data leaves the EU.

**Push services, if you turn notifications on.** A notification to a browser has to travel
through whoever made that browser — Google, Apple or Mozilla. They relay a message encrypted to
keys only your own browser holds, so what they pass on they cannot read. Turning notifications
off, or forgetting the device, ends that.

The full list, and what each one is for, is in the [Lore Spawner subprocessor
list](subprocessors.md). If it ever has to change, that list and this page will say so, and who
they are, before it does.

The exception every service has to name: if we are ever legally required to hand something over,
we will, and we will tell you unless we are forbidden to.

## The AI, and what it is told

There are two different AIs here and they hold different things.

**The AI at the fire** is a language model this campfire can be pointed at, which can play or
tell a character you have handed to it and can talk with you about a world. It is off unless
the campfire has been given a model to use. When it runs, what it is sent is the part of the
world it is acting in — the events, the places, the characters, and what you have said to it —
and that goes to whoever serves that model. Where that is not a model Kaperkunde runs itself,
that provider is a processor and is named in the
[subprocessor list](subprocessors.md), which is the honest place to look before you turn it on.

**An assistant you connect yourself** is the other: an outside app you allow over OAuth, which
then acts as you within the limits you set when you allow it. It reads what you can read, and it
writes only where you have ticked a box saying it may. The connection is a token held by that
app, not a copy of your password, and you can take it back at any time from the AI page in your
account. What that app does with what it reads is between you and whoever makes it — it is your
assistant, not ours.

Kaperkunde does not train anything on your worlds, your conversations or your writing, and
neither AI changes that.

## Taking it with you, or taking it down

Every world you own can be exported from the app itself, at any time, as one file you can keep
and put back. That is the fastest way to have a copy of your work, and it needs nobody's help.
It is also how Kaperkunde's company-wide export promise is kept here — see
[§4.1 of the Privacy Policy](../privacy.md#41-what-we-do-instead).

For the rest — your account and everything attached to it — email **privacy@kaperkun.de** and we
will send you a machine-readable copy, or delete it, whichever you ask for. Either way you hear
back within 30 days, and normally a great deal sooner than that.

Deleting an account removes it and the work attached to it. Where your writing is woven into a
world other people are still playing in, we will talk it through with you before anything goes,
so we take out what is yours without collapsing someone else's story.
