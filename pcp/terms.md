## 1. What PCP is

PCP is a self-hosted gateway between an assistant and the servers you use. You install it on a
machine you control, add your servers and the secrets they need, and connect an assistant to it
with an API token.

Kaperkunde publishes the code. There is no Kaperkunde-run PCP, no account with Kaperkunde and
no service to sign up for. Nothing you do with PCP reaches Kaperkunde.
