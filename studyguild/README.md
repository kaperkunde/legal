# Study Guild — Legal

Legal documentation for **Study Guild**, the shared flashcard community at

**Effective date: 28 September 2026** · [Repository overview](../README.md)

## Documents

|----------|-------------|
| [Privacy Notice](privacy.md) | What Study Guild holds, why, how long, who else ever sees it — and that guilds are public |

Study Guild is free. There is **no acceptance gate** — these documents are published rather
than agreed to, and creating an account is what accepts them; the sign-up form says so and
links to them.

## How these are published

Study Guild renders these files directly. The app carries this repository as a git submodule
at `legal/` and renders the markdown on its pages, so `/terms`, `/privacy` and
`/subprocessors` on the site are this folder, and `/privacy/kaperkunde` is the company-wide
policy beside them. Changing a document here and bumping the submodule is what publishes it.
